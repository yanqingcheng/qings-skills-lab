# Generate creative ideas

1. Run [scripts/generate_stimulus.py](scripts/generate_stimulus.py) with Python 3 to generate a long random alphanumeric string, then read it.
2. Look beyond its surface for sub-patterns, special numbers, rhythms, shapes, or anything that sparks an association.
3. Use your judgment to turn those associations into creative directions for the task at hand. Keep what fits; let go of forced connections.

The string is only inspiration. Keep it and copied fragments out of the final response and design.
